Our Story

Small-batch baking, big-hearted moments.

We started Sweet Crumbs with a simple belief: a good cake can turn an ordinary day into a memory. Our bakers work in small batches, balancing classic comfort with modern flavours and clean, considered decoration.

From a first birthday to a golden anniversary, we make each order with the same care we would bring to our own table.
